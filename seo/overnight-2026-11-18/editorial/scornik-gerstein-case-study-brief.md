# Scornik Gerstein LLP case study: brief

**Purpose:** turn the business's one commissioned project into proof, without inventing anything. Use this brief to collect the facts from the owner's records and, with permission, from the client. Then write the case study (about 800–1,200 words) to the structure below.

**Status:** not started. **Gate:** written permission from Scornik Gerstein LLP to publish, and their approval of the final text. A draft request is in [backlink opportunities](../13-backlink-opportunities.md#drafts-for-owner-approval-not-sent) and has not been sent.

## What is already verified (18 Nov 2026)

- Scornik Gerstein LLP is a London law firm regulated by the SRA (number 565232), at Staple Inn, London WC1V 7QH.
- Their production site is `https://www.scornik.com/`, served by Vercel and built with Next.js. Its robots.txt disallows `/studio`, which suggests a headless CMS studio, though that is unconfirmed.
- The live hero reads "Your London Solicitors for…", matching the screenshot on the Chinvat Bridge homepage.
- The site does not currently credit its builder.

## Questions for the owner (from project records)

1. When did the project start and go live? Was it a new site or a rebuild?
2. What did the firm ask for, in their words? What was wrong with what they had?
3. Who were the site's audiences (individuals, businesses, specific practice areas), and what did each need to find?
4. What did we build: pages, CMS (which one?), contact or consultation flow, anything bespoke?
5. Which constraints shaped it? (SRA transparency rules on publishing prices and complaints information, accessibility, the partners' time to supply content, an existing domain or email setup.)
6. What technical SEO work was part of the build: redirects from the old site, structured data (LegalService or Attorney), titles and metadata, performance?
7. What can the firm now do that it couldn't before, such as editing its own pages or publishing news?
8. Is there any measured change the firm is willing to share (enquiries, page speed, rankings)? **Only include figures the firm provides and approves**, with their time period.
9. Would someone at the firm give a one- or two-sentence comment, in their own words, with their name and role? **Never write one for them.**

## Suggested structure

1. **The firm and what it needed** (two short paragraphs).
2. **What we built**: the site's structure for its audiences, the editing setup, the consultation route.
3. **The details that mattered**: regulatory content, accessibility, speed, search foundations.
4. **The result**: what the firm can now do, plus any approved figures with dates.
5. **In their words** (optional, only if supplied).
6. **Links:** the live site, our [custom software](/services/custom-software) and [SEO](/services/seo) pages, and the contact CTA.

## SEO metadata (proposed)

- **URL:** `/work/scornik-gerstein` (with a small Work index) or `/insights/scornik-gerstein-case-study`
- **Title:** Scornik Gerstein LLP: Website Design and Build for a London Law Firm — Chinvat Bridge
- **Description:** How we designed and built the website of Scornik Gerstein LLP, a London law firm: its structure, editing and search foundations, and what it does for the firm.
- **Schema:** Article (or CreativeWork) with `about` → the firm as an `Organization` (`sameAs` their site); BreadcrumbList.
- **Internal links in:** homepage Recent work (link the case study, keep the external link to the live site), `/services/custom-software` ("How we build"), the future law-firm page.

## Rules

- No invented figures, quotes or outcomes.
- Don't describe the firm's clients or matters.
- Screenshots: use the existing homepage capture or new ones the firm approves.
- Ask the firm to add a "Website by Chinvat Bridge" credit at the same time; it's an earned, relevant link (optional for them).
