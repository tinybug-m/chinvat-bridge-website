## What not to write

- **Head-term explainers** ("what is business process automation", "what is an LLM"). Vendors and encyclopaedias own them, and their readers aren't buyers.
- **"Best AI agencies UK" style lists.** A crowded tactic that spends trust.
- **Platform tutorials** (Zapier, n8n, Xero API, Looker Studio). Vendor docs dominate, and they would claim expertise that isn't evidenced.
- **City pages and vertical pages without proof.** No dealership, van-hire or law-firm landing page until there is work to show (see [positioning](01-business-positioning.md#verticals-that-were-tested)).
- **A second "where to start with AI" piece.** The existing article covers it; refresh it rather than compete with it.

## Cannibalisation check

| Pair | Risk | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| /services/ai-integration vs "Where AI can save your business time" | Low | Service page targets "AI consultancy/integration" (commercial); article targets "where AI saves time" (informational). They link to each other. |
| /services/ai-integration vs "How to choose an AI consultant" | Medium | Article targets *choosing and hiring* (consultant cost, questions to ask); the service page targets the service itself. Keep the article's title and H1 about choosing, not "AI consultancy UK". |
| /services/workflow-automation vs "What should you automate first?" | Low | Commercial vs "what to automate" informational |
| /services/workflow-automation vs "How to connect business systems" | Medium | Article is problem-led ("systems that don't talk to each other") and explains options; the service page sells the work. The article links up with "workflow automation and integration". |
| /services/custom-software vs "When should a business build custom software?" vs "Outgrown your spreadsheets?" | Medium | Three distinct intents: service, build-vs-buy decision, spreadsheet-specific problem. Each links to the others with distinct anchors. |
| /services/seo vs /pricing vs "How much does SEO cost in the UK?" | Medium | /pricing = our packages (transactional); the article = market cost explainer (investigation); the service page = the service. The article links to /pricing with "our plans", never "SEO pricing UK". |
| /services/seo vs "What a technical SEO audit should include" | Low | Audit-specific investigation |
